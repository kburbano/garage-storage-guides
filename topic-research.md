### Winner

**Best garage slatwall systems: Proslat vs Gladiator vs StoreWALL** — published as `garage-slatwall-proslat-vs-gladiator.html`.

| | Demand | Ticket | Competition |
|---|---|---|---|
| Score | High | Medium | Medium |

- **Demand:** Google autocomplete for “best slatwall for garage”, “best slatwall system”, “best slatwall system reddit”, and “garage slatwall” plus panels, installation, Costco, and shelves.
- **Ticket:** Top pick is the Proslat 4×8 section at $199.99. A two-section starter wall is about $400 before hooks. StoreWALL was $349 for 16 sq ft. Flow Wall’s 48 sq ft pack was listed at $499.99 and out of stock. Gladiator’s GearWall 2-pack was $129.99 at Home Depot (a search snippet had said $119.99; the live Home Depot page was $129.99).
- **Competition:** Niche blogs (bayandbench, cleverhomestorage, steelslat) rather than a Home Depot category page or Wirecutter owning this exact comparison, plus a lot of Reddit decision threads.
- **Fit:** Wall systems, and it links to the overhead, bike, and ladder guides already on the site.

Same-night re-check before publish:

- Proslat 88102 white was $199.99 at Amazon (in stock, 4.4 stars, 1,022 ratings), Home Depot, and proslat.com (listed down from $239.99).
- Gladiator GAWP082PBY had no featured offer on Amazon, so this site does not quote an Amazon price. Home Depot was $129.99. Current Amazon bullets and a Gladiator Team Home Depot answer both say 50 lb per square foot. Home Depot’s Gladiator Team also said screws are not included.
- StoreWALL B0863CNJZJ was $349.00 ($21.81 per square foot), in stock, sold by Vennder, usually ships in 2–3 days. InstallStrips and screws are extra.
- Flow Wall’s white 48 sq ft pack was $499.99 and out of stock on flowwall.com. The Amazon silver listing (ASIN B003UZ0F3Y) was unavailable.
